## App Store Optimization (ASO) Tips

### Keywords Strategy
- Primary: umrah, hajj, pilgrimage, travel booking
- Secondary: islamic travel, makkah, madinah, visa application
- Long-tail: book umrah packages, islamic pilgrimage app, hajj booking

### Rating & Reviews
- Target: 4.5+ stars minimum
- Encourage user reviews after successful bookings
- Respond to all reviews professionally
- Address negative reviews promptly

- Adapt content for regional preferences
- Use local payment methods
